# Junteng Liu

First-year PhD candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group** (Hong Kong University of Science and Technology), advised by **Professor Junxian He**. I graduated with a **B.Eng.** from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University** in June 2024. My research focuses on **natural language processing and machine learning**, with particular interests in **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**, **hallucination in vision-language models (VLM)**, and **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**.
